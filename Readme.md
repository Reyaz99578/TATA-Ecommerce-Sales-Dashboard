## 🎯 The Business Question

> Which **states**, **product categories** and **payment modes** drive sales and profit, and in which **months** and **sub-categories** is profit weak?

## 🔍 What I Found

- 🏆 **Maharashtra** is the top state by sales, followed by **Madhya Pradesh** and **Uttar Pradesh**
- 💳 **Cash on Delivery** is the most used payment mode at **44%**, then UPI (21%), Debit Card (13%), Credit Card (12%) and EMI (10%)
- 🖨️ **Printers** and **Bookcases** are the most profitable sub-categories

## ✅ What I Recommend

| Finding | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Maharashtra leads in sales | Focus marketing and inventory on top states |
| COD is 44% of orders | Promote UPI to reduce COD dependency and return risk |
| Printers and Bookcases earn the most profit | Invest more in these high-profit sub-categories |
| Some months show low or negative profit | Review pricing and discounts in those months |

## 📊 What the Dashboard Includes

- KPI cards: sales, profit, quantity and average order value (AOV)
- Sales by state
- Quantity share by category and by payment mode (donut charts)
- Monthly profit trend
- Profit by sub-category
- Top customers by amount
- Quarter slicer and city filter
